About the hospital

Light Shade Hospital and Maternity Home is a hospital and maternity home in Ayobo, Lagos. It brings general medical care, emergency care, maternity services, diagnostics and therapy services together under one roof.

The hospital is open 24 hours, so you can call at any time.
